A full-width choice button for the one question on an onboarding or setup screen.

- `white` fill on `paper`, `stroke-ink` `navy` border, `radius-lg`, 56px tall, 17px `label` weight 700, left-aligned.
- Selected: `navy` fill with `on-navy` text, a `mustard` check at the right, and `aria-pressed="true"`.
- The consumer provides two to four options, short enough for one line, and the question above them in `display-l`.
- Single choice by default; a multi-choice screen says "Pick all that apply" under the question.
- Don't add descriptions under options or put more than four on a screen.
- Every option on the screen is the same 56px height, including the selected one and any "coming soon" options.
