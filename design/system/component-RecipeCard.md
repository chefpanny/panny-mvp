The card for one creator recipe in a dinner feed: what it is, who made it, and how ready you are to cook it.

- `white` card, `stroke-ink` `navy` border, `radius-md`, `shadow-card`.
- Top: a `parchment` media area with the dish image or the video placeholder (never an autoplaying embed in a feed). The night sits top left as a `navy` pill; the goal tag and at most one filter tag sit bottom left; the swap control sits top right.
- Below, in order: the creator credit in `caption` ("By @handle"), the dish name in `title`, and one meta line in `chip` type: time and pantry match ("25 min · You have 6 of 10").
- The consumer provides the image, creator handle, title, minutes, pantry counts and fit level.
- Keep it to those three lines. Nutrition, evidence and steps belong on the recipe page.
