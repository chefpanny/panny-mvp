A fat, outlined softened rectangle that starts the one action a screen is for.

- **Primary:** `cherry` fill, white `label` text. One per screen.
- **Secondary:** `white` fill, `navy` text, for the alternative ("Swap it," "Skip this week").
- Both: `stroke-ink` `navy` border, `shadow-press` (offset down and right), `label` type, 56px tall, `radius-lg`. On press the shadow shrinks to 2px 2px and the button moves 2px down and right.
- The consumer provides the label (a verb phrase, two or three words: "Let's cook," "Add 6 items to list") and the action.
- Don't put two primary buttons on one screen, use icons without a label, set navy text on `cherry`, or set a button label in the script face.
