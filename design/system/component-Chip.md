A pill for filters and quick picks; selected chips invert to `navy`.

- **Filter chip:** `paper` fill, `stroke-ink` `navy` border, `chip` type, 48px tall. Selected: `navy` fill, `on-navy` text, weight 700, `aria-pressed="true"`.
- **Tag:** a non-interactive `parchment` pill with no border, for facts about a recipe (Nut-free, One pan).
- **Filter tag:** a `mint` pill with a `navy` border, weight 500, for audience filters (Kid-friendly).
- The consumer provides the label (one or two words) and the selected state.
- Keep chip rows to one line on mobile; more than five chips means a filter sheet instead.
- Don't show selection by color alone, or use `cherry` chips except for an allergen warning.
