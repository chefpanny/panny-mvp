Shows how strong the evidence is for a food's role in the user's goal, so a food with an FDA-authorized claim never looks equally proven as one with early research.

- A pill: a 18px `navy` disc with the tier letter, then the label, 12px weight 700, `stroke-ink` border, `radius-pill`, 24px tall.
- **A · FDA-authorized claim** on `mint` (green). **B · Strong research** on `mustard` (yellow). **C · Early research** on `white`.
- Use it on every food on the goal's foods screen, on each "How it works" item on Why it fits, and as "Evidence A" in the week's food strip.
- Pair the first use on a screen with a legend and a "How we grade evidence" link.
- The consumer provides the tier from the evidence rubric; copy never sets or upgrades a tier, and the tier never appears on a recipe as a whole.
