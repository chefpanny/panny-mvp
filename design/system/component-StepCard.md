The recipe index card that holds a recipe's steps (and, in the same shape, the shopping list).

- `white` card, `stroke-ink` `navy` border, `radius-sm`, `shadow-card`, a 10px `cherry` top band, `rule-blue` ruled lines every 28px.
- Header row in `eyebrow` ("RECIPE FOR · MONDAY") with the creator credit at the right in `caption`.
- Steps are numbered, `body-l`, and every step names each ingredient with its amount, scaled to the chosen servings, in bold on a `parchment` highlight: "Warm **2 tbsp olive oil**."
- The consumer provides the steps as text segments with amounts marked, and the servings.
- Don't write a step that says "the oil" or "the beans" without the amount, and don't put more than one recipe on a card.
