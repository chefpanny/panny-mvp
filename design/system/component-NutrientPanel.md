Per-serving nutrition for the nutrients that matter to the user's goal. For healthy LDL: fiber (with the soluble share), protein, saturated fat and sodium.

- **Card row:** three `paper` tiles inside a recipe card: value in `display` 19px, label in `caption` ("12 g fiber," "16 g protein," "3 g sat fat").
- **Recipe panel:** a 2×2 grid of `white` tiles with a `navy` border: value in `display` 22px, label, a note ("incl. 3 g soluble"), and the % Daily Value as a number and a `mustard` bar on `parchment`.
- An `eyebrow` "PER SERVING" header and a caption that says where numbers come from (the recipe's amounts plus USDA data; Daily Values from FDA).
- The consumer provides values per serving and the goal's nutrient set; the panel never grades, colors red/green or labels a number "good" or "bad."
- Don't show calories by default, or more than four nutrients.
